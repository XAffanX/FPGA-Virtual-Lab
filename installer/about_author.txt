FPGA Virtual Lab 1.1.0
======================

Created by XAffanX
GitHub:  https://github.com/XAffanX
Project: https://github.com/XAffanX/FPGA-Virtual-Lab

Virtual Digilent FPGA boards (virtual_basys3, virtual_nexys4ddr) for
bitstreams built in AMD Vivado. Load a .bit, or program the virtual board
straight from Vivado's Hardware Manager, then use the switches, buttons,
LEDs and seven-segment display as on the real board.

Requirements: Windows 10/11 (64-bit). AMD Vivado must be installed to load
.bit files. The Icarus Verilog simulator (free software, GPL) is included;
its source is at https://github.com/steveicarus/iverilog

Your design runs in a simulator - no real hardware is configured.
